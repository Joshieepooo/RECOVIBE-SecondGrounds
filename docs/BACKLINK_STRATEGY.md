# RecoVibe: Academic & Institutional Backlink Strategy
**Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) Biñan Campus**
*Author: RecoVibe Development Team | Defense & Production Roadmap*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

Search engines prioritize high-trust, authoritative backlinks when determining topical credibility and search rankings. For an educational web platform like **RecoVibe**, backlinks from `.edu.ph`, accredited university organizations, civic partners, and academic repositories carry extraordinary domain authority (DA/DR).

The objective of this Backlink Strategy is to establish a strong institutional backlink profile that:
1. Solidifies RecoVibe as the recognized official event and seminar portal for PUP Biñan Campus.
2. Increases organic discovery when students, faculty, or guests search for campus events, student organization seminars, or university calendars.
3. Establishes academic provenance ahead of capstone defense and deployment.

---

## 2. Institutional Backlink Pillars

### Pillar A: Official PUP University Subdomains (.edu.ph) — Highest Authority
Backlinks from `.edu.ph` domains carry the highest search trust. 

| Source / Placement | Target URL on RecoVibe | Strategy & Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PUP Biñan Campus Branch Page** (`pup.edu.ph/binan/`) | `https://yourdomain.com/` | Add a featured resource link under "Student Services" or "Campus Events & Activities". |
| **PUP Student Affairs & Services (SAS)** | `https://yourdomain.com/UserStudent/StudentLogin.html` | Embed RecoVibe as the primary portal for student organization event accreditations. |
| **PUP Central Student Council (CSC) Portal** | `https://yourdomain.com/UserStudent/Events.html` | Feature RecoVibe in official CSC memoranda, student handbooks, and announcements. |
| **PUP Academic Calendar Noticeboard** | `https://yourdomain.com/UserStudent/Calendar.html` | Cite RecoVibe as the interactive synchronized calendar companion to the official university calendar. |

---

### Pillar B: Recognized Student Organizations & Department Portals
Student organizations (such as IBITS) host active social channels, documentation, and external project sites.

1. **Organization Documentation & GitHub Repositories**:
   - Student organizations maintaining public repositories, event landing pages, or Linktree profiles should embed direct links to RecoVibe event registration pages.
   - *Anchor text example*: `Register on RecoVibe`, `PUP Biñan Events Portal`.
2. **Organization Social Linktrees & Link-in-Bio Services**:
   - Every affiliated student organization (e.g. IBITS, CSC, Department Councils) adds `https://yourdomain.com/` to their Bio.fm / Linktree profile as their official event hub.
3. **Event Posters & Digital Press Releases**:
   - Shortlinks or QR codes leading directly to canonical RecoVibe event URLs (e.g., `/UserStudent/Events.html`) embedded on digital promotional banners.

---

### Pillar C: Speaker & Industry Partner Backlinks
External guest speakers, tech mentors, and corporate seminar partners frequently publish recaps of their talks.

1. **Speaker Profile Links**:
   - Provide invited speakers with pre-written event announcement copy and backlinks to their specific seminar page on RecoVibe.
   - *Example*: *"I will be presenting at PUP Biñan Campus on AI Architectures. Register through RecoVibe: https://yourdomain.com/UserStudent/Events.html"*.
2. **Industry Sponsor & Co-Organizer Portals**:
   - Collaborating companies and sponsors include RecoVibe in their community outreach and CSR program highlights.

---

### Pillar D: Academic Repositories, Research Defense & Capstone Citation
Academic research papers and capstone repositories yield long-term, indestructible authoritative citations.

1. **Institutional Repository & Digital Library**:
   - Ensure the final manuscript in the PUP Biñan Library database references the canonical production URL.
2. **Zenodo / ResearchGate / IEEE Student Branch**:
   - If submitting research papers or tech reports regarding RecoVibe’s AI recommendation algorithms or conflict detection logic, include the permanent platform link in the abstract and dataset citations.
3. **GitHub Project Repository**:
   - Maintain the GitHub repository homepage and README pointing directly to the deployed platform.

---

### Pillar E: Local Government & Community Outreach Partners
Events that engage the broader Biñan community:

1. **Biñan City Youth Development Office (YDO)**:
   - Partner on public seminars, career expos, and community outreach events with reciprocal links from city cultural/educational bulletins.
2. **Local Tech Meetups & Community Hubs**:
   - List university-hosted public events on local developer and student hackathon directories.

## 3. Anchor Text Distribution Strategy

To ensure natural search engine ranking and avoid algorithmic penalties:

| Anchor Type | Percentage | Target Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Branded** | 45% | `RecoVibe`, `RecoVibe Portal`, `RecoVibe PUP Biñan` |
| **Exact / Topical** | 25% | `PUP Biñan campus events`, `PUP Biñan event calendar`, `Student event registration` |
| **Entity / URL** | 20% | `https://yourdomain.com/`, `yourdomain.com/FirstPage.html` |
| **Generic / Call-to-Action** | 10% | `View campus calendar`, `Sign in to portal`, `Browse events` |
